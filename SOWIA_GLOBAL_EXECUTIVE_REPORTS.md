# SOWIA GLOBAL EXECUTIVE REPORTS

On-demand reporting across the private global intelligence, alerts, forecasts, decisions, learning, action governance and performance records.

Exports:
- PDF executive report;
- JSON report with section details;
- CSV of summary indicators.

Reports are generated on demand and are not stored or emailed automatically. Internal user and record identifiers are omitted from exported sections. Currency totals remain separated by currency. Evidence levels and impact notes are not independently verified. This is not a financial audit, legal opinion or ESG certification.

No database migration is required.
