# SOW IA — dossier de visibilité internationale

> Document de travail destiné aux présentations, candidatures à des événements, incubateurs, accélérateurs, investisseurs et partenaires. À relire et adapter avant toute diffusion externe.

## 1. Positionnement — français

**SOW IA est une plateforme d'intelligence artificielle conçue pour aider les particuliers et les professionnels à transformer l'information en actions structurées.**

Le socle actuel réunit un assistant IA, l'analyse et la création de documents PDF, une mémoire et un historique, une recherche web optionnelle, des espaces Personnel et Professionnel, ainsi que des outils en développement pour la recherche de marchés, la qualification de partenaires et le suivi d'opportunités commerciales.

La vision est de rendre l'intelligence et les outils de développement accessibles aux entrepreneurs, startups, PME et organisations, quel que soit leur pays ou leur stade de développement.

**Promesse :** observer les marchés, identifier des opportunités, préparer des décisions et organiser le développement — avec des sources consultables et une validation humaine pour les actions engageantes.

## 2. Positionnement — English

**SOW IA is an AI platform designed to help individuals and professionals turn information into structured action.**

Its current foundation brings together an AI assistant, PDF analysis and document creation, conversation history and memory, optional web research, Personal and Professional workspaces, and evolving tools for market research, partner qualification and business opportunity tracking.

Our vision is to make practical intelligence and business-development tools more accessible to entrepreneurs, startups, SMEs and organizations across countries and stages of growth.

**Our approach:** observe markets, identify opportunities, prepare decisions and organize growth — using traceable sources and human approval for consequential actions.

## 4. Audiences and use cases

### Startups and founders
- Structure research before entering a new market.
- Prepare company documents, briefs and proposals.
- Organize partner and opportunity pipelines.
- Prepare negotiation drafts for human review.

### Incubators and accelerators
- Support founders with research and document workflows.
- Help teams structure market-entry questions and partnership preparation.
- Explore workshops or pilot programs, subject to agreed scope.

### Investors and strategic partners
- Review a product demonstration and roadmap.
- Discuss potential pilots, distribution, integrations or strategic partnerships.
- Assess product maturity, security, market fit and measurable outcomes through due diligence.

### Technology events and innovation communities
- Apply for startup showcases, demo sessions, exhibition spaces, pitch competitions and community partnerships.
- Offer a focused demonstration rather than making unsupported performance claims.

## 5. International outreach priorities

The platform is intended for a global audience. Initial research priorities include:

- North America: United States, Canada and wider startup ecosystems.
- East Asia: China, South Korea and Taiwan.
- South and Southeast Asia: India, Singapore, Indonesia, including Bali.
- Middle East: United Arab Emirates, including Dubai, and neighboring markets.
- Europe: France, Switzerland and wider European ecosystems.
- Africa: Senegal and other established and emerging innovation hubs.
- Latin America and other emerging economies worldwide.

This is a research scope, not a claim that SOW IA is already launched, represented or partnered in every listed market. Local regulations, language, infrastructure and distribution requirements must be assessed country by country.
